# 0.1.0-alpha.3

First public single-player alpha for Minecraft Java 26.3 and Fabric.

- Iron Pistol with left-click semi-automatic fire, right-click aiming, centered sights, recoil, sound, and shot particles.
- Server-side hit selection with 8 base damage, a 64-block range, nearest-target selection, solid-surface obstruction, and a half-second firing cooldown.
- Unlimited ammunition for this alpha.
- Wearable night-vision goggles with green lenses, brightened dark environments, and a green monochrome post-processing filter.
- Automatic starter equipment in new and existing worlds, replacement after death, Keep Inventory retention, and pending delivery for full inventories.
- Creative Combat/search entries and native item models.

Verification covered standalone play and coexistence with Ashen Arena, attack/use inputs, aiming zoom and release, hit damage, cooldown, range, obstruction, respawn equipment, full inventories, dark-enclosure visibility, green filter activation/removal, and potion preservation. Item and goggles appearance were checked in gameplay.

Single-player is supported. Multiplayer, LAN, dedicated servers, shader packs, and other mod combinations have not been validated.
